Privacy Policy

Student Seva Maharashtra

1. Introduction

Welcome to Student Seva Maharashtra. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website and services.

2. Information We May Collect

When you contact us or request assistance with a form or application, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, mobile number, educational details, application details, or documents required for the requested service.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided by users may be used only for providing the requested assistance, communicating with the user, completing form-filling services, and responding to service-related questions.

4. Documents and Personal Information

Users should provide only the documents and information necessary for the requested service. We do not ask users to share passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, ATM PINs, or other confidential authentication information.

5. WhatsApp Communication

Some services on this website may open WhatsApp to allow users to contact us. Information shared through WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices and policies.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may provide links to official government or educational websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or services of third-party websites.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for students and applicants. Where a minor requires assistance, information should be provided with the knowledge and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our services, you can contact Student Seva Maharashtra through the contact options provided on our website.
