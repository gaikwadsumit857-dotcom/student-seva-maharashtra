About Student Seva Maharashtra

Student Seva Maharashtra is a student-focused online platform designed to make common educational services and online application assistance easier and more accessible.

We provide assistance with selected student services, scholarship forms, university examination forms and online applications.

Student Seva Maharashtra is an independent assistance platform and is not a government website.
